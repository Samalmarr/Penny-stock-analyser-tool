# Penny Stock Analyser — OTC Dilution, Bankruptcy & 3-Year Financial Checker

Live Tool: **[https://pennystockanalysertool.vercel.app/](https://pennystockanalysertool.vercel.app/)**

**Penny Stock Analyser** is a free single-stock due-diligence terminal for researching OTC, Pink Sheet, and small-cap stocks (`PLUG`, `ACHR`, `NIO`, `AITX`, `FNMA`, and more) with zero login or paywall.

---

## Key Features

- **Bankruptcy & Q-Suffix Warnings:** Automatically detects 5-letter `-Q` bankruptcy symbols (`BBBYQ`, `SHLDQ`) and highlights Chapter 11 / Chapter 7 equity cancellation risks.
- **Past 3 Years Financials:** Pulls 3-year annual **Turnover (Revenue)**, **Net Profit / (Loss)**, and **Total Debt** directly from market fundamentals feeds.
- **Dilution & Red-Flag Assessment:** Evaluates share-structure dilution risks, reverse-split traps, and toxic convertible debt warning signs.
- **Smart Jurisdiction & Filing Portals:** Automatically distinguishes US domestic issuers (**SEC EDGAR**) from Canadian dual-listed OTC tickers (**SEDAR+ / SEC Rule 12g3-2(b)**) and links directly to verified **Yahoo Finance**, **Google Finance**, and **OTC Markets** pages.
- **Direct Shareable Ticker URLs:** Inspect any stock directly using `?ticker=SYMBOL` (for example: [`/?ticker=PLUG`](https://pennystockanalysertool.vercel.app/?ticker=PLUG) or [`/?ticker=ACHR`](https://pennystockanalysertool.vercel.app/?ticker=ACHR)).
- **Live Data Only:** Never generates synthetic placeholder prices or simulated charts.
